MORGAN Travelers

Hong Kong transit map & trip planner. Routes use the public GTFS graph (RAPTOR) with Octopus adult fare estimates. Search supports @MTR, @LRT, and @Bus filters.

Data: MTR open data · Protomaps · OpenStreetMap contributors.

Contribute

Bus paths are drawn live from the road network and can miss real alignments (e.g. Mei Tung Street vs the bus terminus). On desktop, you can load a calculated path and edit turning points for moderators to review.

Desktop only · mouse/trackpad recommended.
