Six cohesive modules form the MTR domain layer consumed by routing and UI code:
- `mtrLayer.js` is the MapLibre entry point: it lazily fetches four GeoJSON sources (`stations`, `exits`, `platforms`, `lrt-platforms`) from `${BASE_URL}mtr/`, registers them as hidden sources/layers, and exposes `setRouteStationCodes(map, opts)` to filter exits/platforms by itinerary via `neverFilter`/`neverPlatformFilter`. It also resolves a plan stop to a concrete platform (heavy rail or LRT) with fallbacks for Tin Wing indoor platforms and nearest-OSM-LRT matching.
- `mtrStations.js` holds the static `MTR_STATIONS` array plus search/snap helpers (`searchMtrStationsLocal`, `snapToMtrStation`) and merges live GeoJSON station pins while honouring locked access-pin overrides from `overrides.js`.
- `mtrBusData.js` loads MTR Bus (LRT feeder) CSVs through a three-tier fallback chain (bundled `public/data/*.csv` → `/eta/mtr-open` proxy → direct `opendata.mtr.com.hk`), parses them with an in-file CSV parser, and caches routes/stops behind `ensureMtrBusData`.
- `mtrColors.js` maps GTFS options to official MTR brand hex codes via short-code, long-name regex hints, and mode/agency heuristics; it also detects Light Rail vs HK Tramways.
- `mtrInterchange.js` encodes historical KCR↔MTR interchange policy: lists of long-transfer stations, integrated hubs, Admiralty same-period cohorts, free cross-station links (CEN↔HOK, TST↔ETS, MOK↔MKK), and utilities like `isLegacyKcrMtrInterchange`, `isIndoorMtrInterchangeWalk`, `isSameMtrStation`.
- `mtrLineOrder.js` declares canonical station sequences per line (`MTR_LINE_ORDER`), branch variants (`MTR_LINE_VARIANTS` for EAL LOW/LMC, TKL POA/LHP), and direction/label helpers.
Dependency direction is one-way: `mtrInterchange.js` imports `detectMtrLineCode` from `mtrColors.js`; all other files are independent leaf modules that export pure functions/constants consumed elsewhere. No module imports another sibling except the color→interchange link.
